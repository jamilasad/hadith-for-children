My First 24 Hadith

Words of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ for little hearts

This book belongs to:

For parents & teachers

How to use this book

  1. Look at the picture together and ask: "What do you think is happening?"
  2. Read the Arabic slowly. Let your child repeat it phrase by phrase. Don't rush — one hadith can take a whole week.
  3. Read the translation, then the "What does it mean for me?" box, written for a 7-year-old.
  4. Turn the page — "Words to learn": every word of the hadith has its own card with a picture to hang the meaning on. Cover the English and see if your child can say the meaning from the picture. Repeated words appear once.
  5. "Now read it in order": the coloured chips run right-to-left, like the Arabic, so the child can rebuild the whole sentence from the words they just learned.
  6. Let's try it! Do the little action the same day, and tick the star chart at the back.

Good to know

  • Hadith text, narrators and sources follow your original worksheet (Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim).
  • The worksheet's hadith #8 repeats #1, so it is left out; this book has 24 hadith, numbered in order.
  • Some hadith mention the Fire, prison, or Shaytan. The child's explanation keeps the true meaning but frames it gently, focusing on the good action to take.
  • Word-by-word English is meant to help a child map sounds to meaning; some phrases are glossed as a unit. It is not a grammatical parse.

Love for others what you love for yourself

Kindness & Faith · Hadith 1 of 24
“None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.”
Narrated by Anas (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim
عَنْ أَنَسٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: «لَا يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ»
Say it with me: Lā yu'minu aḥadukum ḥattā yuḥibba li-akhīhi mā yuḥibbu li-nafsih.

What does it mean for me?

Do you love getting a yummy treat or a fun toy? A good Muslim wants other people to have nice things too! When we are happy for our friends and share with them, our faith grows big and strong.

Let's try it!

Today, share something you like with a friend or your brother or sister.

Keep your clothes above your ankles

Humility · Hadith 2 of 24
“Whatever of the lower garment extends below the ankles is in the Fire.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: «مَا أَسْفَلَ مِنَ الْكَعْبَيْنِ مِنَ الإِزَارِ فَفِي النَّارِ»
Say it with me: Mā asfala mina l-ka'bayni mina l-izāri fa-fī n-nār.

What does it mean for me?

A long time ago, some proud people let their clothes drag on the ground to show off. The Prophet ﷺ taught boys and men to keep their clothes neat and above the ankles. Being tidy and humble is the way of a good Muslim.

Let's try it!

Look at your trousers or thobe today — are they neat and above your ankles?

Never say bad things about food

Good Manners · Hadith 3 of 24
“The Prophet ﷺ never criticized any food; if he liked it, he ate it, and if he disliked it, he left it.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ: مَا عَابَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ طَعَامًا قَطُّ، إِنِ اشْتَهَاهُ أَكَلَهُ وَإِنْ كَرِهَهُ تَرَكَهُ
Say it with me: Mā 'āba n-Nabiyyu ﷺ ṭa'āman qaṭṭ; in ishtahāhu akalahu, wa in karihahu tarakah.

What does it mean for me?

The Prophet ﷺ never said "Yuck!" about food. If he liked it, he ate it. If he didn't, he just quietly left it. Food is a gift from Allah, so we say Bismillah, say thank you, and never make a fuss.

Let's try it!

At dinner tonight, say "Bismillah" before eating and "Alhamdulillah" after — and no "yuck"!

Send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ

Love for the Prophet · Hadith 4 of 24
“Whoever sends blessings upon me once, Allah will send blessings upon him ten times.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Muslim
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: «مَنْ صَلَّى عَلَيَّ وَاحِدَةً صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ عَشْرًا»
Say it with me: Man ṣallā 'alayya wāḥidatan, ṣalla-llāhu 'alayhi 'ashrā.

What does it mean for me?

When you say "Sallallahu alayhi wa sallam" for our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, Allah sends TEN blessings upon you! One small sentence — and Allah's mercy comes down to you ten times.

Let's try it!

Say "Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad" ten times today. Count on your fingers!

This world is only a short test

Patience · Hadith 5 of 24
“The world is a prison for the believer and a paradise for the disbeliever.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Muslim
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: «الدُّنْيَا سِجْنُ الْمُؤْمِنِ وَجَنَّةُ الْكَافِرِ»
Say it with me: Ad-dunyā sijnu l-mu'mini wa jannatu l-kāfir.

What does it mean for me?

In this world, a believer has rules to follow — like no lying and no being unkind — even when it's hard. It can feel a little like a fence around us. But the real, forever fun is waiting in Jannah (Paradise), which is a million times better than anything here!

Let's try it!

When you have to wait for something today, say "I can be patient — Jannah is worth it!"

Remember Allah all the time

Dhikr · Hadith 6 of 24
“The Prophet ﷺ used to remember Allah at all times.”
Narrated by Aisha (RA) · Muslim
عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: «كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ يَذْكُرُ اللَّهَ عَلَى كُلِّ أَحْيَانِهِ»
Say it with me: Kāna n-Nabiyyu ﷺ yadhkuru-llāha 'alā kulli aḥyānih.

What does it mean for me?

The Prophet ﷺ thought about Allah all day — when he woke up, when he ate, when he walked, and when he went to sleep. We can do it too! Saying "Alhamdulillah", "SubhanAllah" and "Allahu Akbar" keeps our hearts happy and close to Allah.

Let's try it!

Pick three moments today (waking, eating, bedtime) and say "Alhamdulillah" at each one.

Be kind to your neighbours

Neighbours · Hadith 7 of 24
“He will not enter Paradise whose neighbor is not safe from his harm.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Muslim
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: «لَا يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ مَنْ لَا يَأْمَنُ جَارُهُ بَوَائِقَهُ»
Say it with me: Lā yadkhulu l-jannata man lā ya'manu jāruhu bawā'iqah.

What does it mean for me?

Your neighbours are the people who live next door. A good Muslim never hurts them, never makes loud trouble for them, and never breaks their things. Instead we smile, say Salam, and help them. Allah loves a kind neighbour!

Let's try it!

Say "Assalamu alaykum" and smile at a neighbour today, or take them a small treat.

The two rak'ahs before Fajr

Prayer · Hadith 8 of 24
“The two rak'ahs (Sunnah) before Fajr are better than this world and all that is in it.”
Narrated by Aisha (RA) · Muslim
عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: «رَكْعَتَا الْفَجْرِ خَيْرٌ مِنَ الدُّنْيَا وَمَا فِيهَا»
Say it with me: Rak'atā l-fajri khayrun mina d-dunyā wa mā fīhā.

What does it mean for me?

Imagine ALL the toys, sweets, cars and castles in the whole world. Two short rak'ahs of Sunnah prayer before Fajr are better than all of it put together! That's because the reward from Allah lasts forever.

Let's try it!

Wake up for Fajr tomorrow and pray two Sunnah rak'ahs with your parents before the fard prayer.

Never make up words about the Prophet ﷺ

Truthfulness · Hadith 9 of 24
“Whoever lies against me intentionally, let him take his seat in the Fire.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: «مَنْ كَذَبَ عَلَيَّ مُتَعَمِّدًا فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ»
Say it with me: Man kadhaba 'alayya muta'ammidan fal-yatabawwa' maq'adahu mina n-nār.

What does it mean for me?

Lying is always bad, but making up a story and saying "the Prophet ﷺ said it" is a VERY big lie. That's why we only learn hadith from trusted books like Bukhari and Muslim. Always say only what is true!

Let's try it!

Before sharing a hadith, ask: "Which book is it from?" Tell only what is true.

Self-control · Hadith 10 of 24
