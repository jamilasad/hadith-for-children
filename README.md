# My First 24 Hadith

Twenty-four sayings of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) for children, in Arabic with English and Bangla —
picture stories, word-by-word cards, and audio for every hadith and every word.

Read it online: **https://jamilasad.github.io/hadith-for-children/**

- Switch language with the English / বাংলা buttons, and the reciting voice with the male / female buttons.
- Click a hadith, a word card or a word chip to hear it; "Play all words" reads them in order.

Printed editions (A4 and A5) were produced from the same source. Contact: jamilasad1@gmail.com
