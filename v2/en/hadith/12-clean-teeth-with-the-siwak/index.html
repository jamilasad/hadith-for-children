Clean teeth with the siwak

Cleanliness · 12 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: لَوْلَا أَنْ أَشُقَّ عَلَى أُمَّتِي لأَمَرْتُهُمْ بِالسِّوَاكِ عِنْدَ كُلِّ صَلَاةٍ
Say it with me: Lawlā an ashuqqa 'alā ummatī la-amartuhum bis-siwāki 'inda kulli ṣalāh.
“Were it not that I would impose hardship on my nation, I would have commanded them to use the Siwak (tooth-stick) before every prayer.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

The Prophet ﷺ loved clean teeth! He used a special little stick called a siwak. He wanted us to clean our teeth before every prayer, because when we stand before Allah, we should be fresh and clean.
