Allah cares about His rules

Obedience · 20 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغَارُ وَغَيْرَةُ اللَّهِ أَنْ يَأْتِيَ الْمُؤْمِنُ مَا حَرَّمَ اللَّهُ
Say it with me: Inna-llāha yaghāru, wa ghayratu-llāhi an ya'tiya l-mu'minu mā ḥarrama-llāh.
“Allah has a sense of Ghirah (protective honor), and Allah's Ghirah is provoked when a believer commits what Allah has forbidden.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Your parents care a lot when you do something dangerous, because they love you. Allah cares even more when a believer does something He has said is haram (not allowed). His rules are there to protect us, so we follow them with love.
