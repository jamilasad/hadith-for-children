Good things are hard, bad things look easy

Self-control · 10 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: حُجِبَتِ النَّارُ بِالشَّهَوَاتِ وَحُجِبَتِ الْجَنَّةُ بِالْمَكَارِهِ
Say it with me: Ḥujibati n-nāru bish-shahawāt, wa ḥujibati l-jannatu bil-makārih.
“Hellfire is veiled with worldly desires, and Paradise is veiled with hardships.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Sometimes the naughty thing looks fun and easy, like a shiny wrapper. And the good thing feels hard, like getting up early to pray or sharing your last sweet. But behind the hard things is Jannah! Choose the hard-but-good thing, and Allah will reward you.
