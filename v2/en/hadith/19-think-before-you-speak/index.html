Think before you speak

Careful Words · 19 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: إِنَّ الْعَبْدَ لَيَتَكَلَّمُ بِالْكَلِمَةِ مَا يَتَبَيَّنُ فِيهَا يَزِلُّ بِهَا فِي النَّارِ أَبْعَدَ مِمَّا بَيْنَ الْمَشْرِقِ وَالْمَغْرِبِ
Say it with me: Inna l-'abda la-yatakallamu bil-kalimati mā yatabayyanu fīhā, yazillu bihā fi n-nāri ab'ada mimmā bayna l-mashriqi wal-maghrib.
“A servant may speak a word without considering its consequences, for which he sinks into the Fire further than the distance between the East and the West.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Words are powerful! One unkind or untrue word, said without thinking, can cause a LOT of harm — as far as the distance from where the sun rises to where it sets. So before you speak, stop and ask: is it true? is it kind?
