Your mother, your mother, your mother

Parents · 17 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ: جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ فَقَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ مَنْ أَحَقُّ النَّاسِ بِحُسْنِ صَحَابَتِي قَالَ أُمُّكَ ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ أُمُّكَ ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ أُمُّكَ ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ ثُمَّ أَبُوكَ
Say it with me: Jā'a rajulun ilā Rasūli-llāhi ﷺ fa-qāla: Yā Rasūla-llāh, man aḥaqqu n-nāsi bi-ḥusni ṣaḥābatī? Qāla: Ummuk. Qāla: thumma man? Qāla: Ummuk. Qāla: thumma man? Qāla: Ummuk. Qāla: thumma man? Qāla: thumma abūk.
“A man came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and said, "O Messenger of Allah, who among the people is most deserving of my good companionship?" He replied, "Your mother." The man asked, "Then who?" He replied, "Your mother." The man asked, "Then who?" He replied, "Your mother." The man asked, "Then who?" He replied, "Then your father."”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

A man asked the Prophet ﷺ, "Who should I be kindest to?" The answer was "Your mother!" — three times! Then "your father." Your mum carried you, fed you and looked after you, so be extra loving to her, and to your dad too.
