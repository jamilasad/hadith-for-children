Praying at night in Ramadan

Ramadan · 21 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: مَنْ قَامَ رَمَضَانَ إِيمَانًا وَاحْتِسَابًا غُفِرَ لَهُ مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِنْ ذَنْبِهِ
Say it with me: Man qāma Ramaḍāna īmānan wa-ḥtisāban, ghufira lahu mā taqaddama min dhanbih.
“Whoever stands in prayer during Ramadan out of faith and in expectation of reward, his previous sins will be forgiven.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

In Ramadan there is a special night prayer called Taraweeh. If you pray it because you believe in Allah and hope for His reward, Allah wipes away your past mistakes — like a clean whiteboard!
