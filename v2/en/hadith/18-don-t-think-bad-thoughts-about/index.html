Don't think bad thoughts about others

Good Thinking · 18 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: إِيَّاكُمْ وَالظَّنَّ فَإِنَّ الظَّنَّ أَكْذَبُ الْحَدِيثِ
Say it with me: Iyyākum waẓ-ẓann, fa-inna ẓ-ẓanna akdhabu l-ḥadīth.
“Beware of suspicion, for suspicion is the most false of speech.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

If your friend didn't wave at you, don't think "He doesn't like me anymore!" Maybe he just didn't see you. Guessing bad things about people without knowing is called suspicion, and it's usually wrong. Always think the best of others.
