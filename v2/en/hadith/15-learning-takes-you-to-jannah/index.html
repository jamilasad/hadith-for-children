Learning takes you to Jannah

Knowledge · 15 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ
Say it with me: Man salaka ṭarīqan yaltamisu fīhi 'ilman, sahhala-llāhu lahu bihi ṭarīqan ila l-jannah.
“Whoever treads a path seeking knowledge therein, Allah will make easy for him a path to Paradise.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Every time you walk to school, open your Qur'an, or learn a new hadith, Allah is making a nice, easy road for you all the way to Jannah. Learning is an adventure that leads to Paradise!
