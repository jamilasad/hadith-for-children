Going to the masjid

Prayer · 11 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: مَنْ غَدَا إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ أَوْ رَاحَ أَعَدَّ اللَّهُ لَهُ فِي الْجَنَّةِ نُزُلًا كُلَّمَا غَدَا أَوْ رَاحَ
Say it with me: Man ghadā ila l-masjidi aw rāḥ, a'adda-llāhu lahu fī l-jannati nuzulan kullamā ghadā aw rāḥ.
“Whoever goes to the mosque in the morning or in the evening, Allah prepares for him a place of hospitality in Paradise every time he goes.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Every single time you walk to the masjid — morning or evening — Allah gets a special welcome ready for you in Jannah. It's like a party being prepared for you each time!
