Every Muslim is precious

Respect · 13 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: كُلُّ الْمُسْلِمِ عَلَى الْمُسْلِمِ حَرَامٌ دَمُهُ وَمَالُهُ وَعِرْضُهُ
Say it with me: Kullu l-muslimi 'ala l-muslimi ḥarām: damuhu, wa māluhu, wa 'irḍuh.
“Every Muslim is sacred to another Muslim: his blood, his wealth, and his honor.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Three things about your Muslim brother or sister are precious: their body (never hit or hurt them), their things (never take or break them), and their good name (never tease or say mean things about them).
