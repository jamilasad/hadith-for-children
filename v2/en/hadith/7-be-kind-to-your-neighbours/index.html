Be kind to your neighbours

Neighbours · 7 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: لَا يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ مَنْ لَا يَأْمَنُ جَارُهُ بَوَائِقَهُ
Say it with me: Lā yadkhulu l-jannata man lā ya'manu jāruhu bawā'iqah.
“He will not enter Paradise whose neighbor is not safe from his harm.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Your neighbours are the people who live next door. A good Muslim never hurts them, never makes loud trouble for them, and never breaks their things. Instead we smile, say Salam, and help them. Allah loves a kind neighbour!
