Cover your yawn

Good Manners · 23 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: التَّثَاؤُبُ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ فَإِذَا تَثَاءَبَ أَحَدُكُمْ فَلْيَرُدَّهُ مَا اسْتَطَاعَ
Say it with me: At-tathā'ubu mina sh-shayṭān, fa-idhā tathā'aba aḥadukum fal-yarudduhu ma-staṭā'.
“Yawning is from Satan; so if one of you yawns, let him suppress it as much as he can.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

A big open-mouth yawn makes us feel lazy and sleepy — and Shaytan loves lazy! So when a yawn comes, try to hold it in, or put your hand over your mouth. Stay awake and ready, especially in prayer.
