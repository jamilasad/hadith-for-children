Remember Allah all the time

Dhikr · 6 of 24
عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ يَذْكُرُ اللَّهَ عَلَى كُلِّ أَحْيَانِهِ
Say it with me: Kāna n-Nabiyyu ﷺ yadhkuru-llāha 'alā kulli aḥyānih.
“The Prophet ﷺ used to remember Allah at all times.”
Narrated by Aisha (RA) · Muslim

What does it mean for me?

The Prophet ﷺ thought about Allah all day — when he woke up, when he ate, when he walked, and when he went to sleep. We can do it too! Saying "Alhamdulillah", "SubhanAllah" and "Allahu Akbar" keeps our hearts happy and close to Allah.
