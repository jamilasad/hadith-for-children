Love for others what you love for yourself

Kindness & Faith · 1 of 24
عَنْ أَنَسٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: لَا يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ
Say it with me: Lā yu'minu aḥadukum ḥattā yuḥibba li-akhīhi mā yuḥibbu li-nafsih.
“None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.”
Narrated by Anas (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Do you love getting a yummy treat or a fun toy? A good Muslim wants other people to have nice things too! When we are happy for our friends and share with them, our faith grows big and strong.
