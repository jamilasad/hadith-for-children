Two light words, heavy on the scale

Dhikr · 16 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: كَلِمَتَانِ خَفِيفَتَانِ عَلَى اللِّسَانِ ثَقِيلَتَانِ فِي الْمِيزَانِ حَبِيبَتَانِ إِلَى الرَّحْمَنِ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ
Say it with me: Kalimatāni khafīfatāni 'ala l-lisān, thaqīlatāni fi l-mīzān, ḥabībatāni ila r-Raḥmān: Subḥāna-llāhi wa bi-ḥamdih, Subḥāna-llāhi l-'Aẓīm.
“Two words are light on the tongue, heavy on the Scales, and beloved to the Most Merciful: 'SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi, SubhanAllahil-Azim' (Glory be to Allah and His is the praise; Glory be to Allah, the Supreme).”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

These two little sentences are super easy to say, but on the Day of Judgement they will be super HEAVY on your scale of good deeds — and Allah loves them! Say them as much as you can.
