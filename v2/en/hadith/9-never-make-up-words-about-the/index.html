Never make up words about the Prophet ﷺ

Truthfulness · 9 of 24
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: مَنْ كَذَبَ عَلَيَّ مُتَعَمِّدًا فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ
Say it with me: Man kadhaba 'alayya muta'ammidan fal-yatabawwa' maq'adahu mina n-nār.
“Whoever lies against me intentionally, let him take his seat in the Fire.”
Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA) · Al-Bukhari & Muslim

What does it mean for me?

Lying is always bad, but making up a story and saying "the Prophet ﷺ said it" is a VERY big lie. That's why we only learn hadith from trusted books like Bukhari and Muslim. Always say only what is true!
